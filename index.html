The College Process

Present at every stage, not just application season.

Strength of curriculum is still the number one factor selective colleges weigh. Brian works with each family from course selection through the final decision letter.

01

Curriculum Planning

Advanced course selection matched to a student's real abilities and intended major, not simply what classmates are taking.

02

Standardized Testing

Diagnostic guidance on SAT vs. ACT fit, one-on-one tutoring, and strategy for which AP scores to report.

03

The College Essay

Personalized, formula-free writing sessions that surface a student's real voice, never a recycled template.

04

Applications & Activities

Refined Common App activity descriptions and resumes that highlight genuine accomplishment.

05

Community Engagement

Volunteer opportunities matched to real interests: meaningful commitment over resume padding.

06

Awards & Recognition

Guidance toward regional, national, and international competitions, especially in less common fields.

07

Application Timing Strategy

Clear-eyed advice on Early Decision, Early Action, Restrictive Early Action, and Rolling admissions, plus which one serves each student best.

08

Teacher & Counselor Brag Sheets

Help preparing the materials that shape strong, specific letters of recommendation.

The College Essay

No formulas. No stock phrases. Just the student's actual voice.

Brian uses metaphor, a compelling hook, and language that moves between longer intellectual passages and short, powerful lines, never a recycled template or a worn-out phrase.

Each student leaves with one strong core essay, thoughtfully adapted for every supplement, so no one rewrites the same story from scratch for each school.
